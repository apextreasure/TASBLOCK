## Video context

- The system assembly clip is an animation, approximately 70 seconds.
- The approximately 14-second assembly and 8-second dismantling clips are timelapses. Do not confuse playback duration with work duration. They show walls/enclosure, not turnkey house completion. The source claims 2.5 hours and 45 minutes, respectively; the default website copy need not repeat those figures.
- Audio has not been transcribed or cleared by this review. Inspect it before public launch; do not fabricate captions.
- All 11 embedded MP4 videos are included: three in public assets and eight in reference-only. The unavailable external clips from slides 16 and 47 are not included.
- Hammer strikes are uncontrolled demonstrations. Flame footage does not establish a fire rating. In the wave-test footage, lower/front wall sections detach under repeated waves; do not edit the footage to imply an unqualified pass.
- D-TACT is optional background for mobile facilities, not a requirement for the homeowner-focused homepage. Do not describe a 2020 patent-pending claim as current patent status.
